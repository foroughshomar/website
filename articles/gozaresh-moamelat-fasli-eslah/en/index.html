Misfiled a quarterly transaction report? Read this firstMisfiled a quarterly transaction report? Read this first

Filed the quarterly transaction report in error? Common mistakes, the difference from VAT, and how to correct them, in this practical guide.

28 October 2026

By Dr. Hamed Molatefi, tax adviser · Reviewed 28 October 2026

Short answer

Do not start a correction by guessing. First match that quarter against the invoices, the books, and the counterparty identifier, so you can see whether the error is in the identifier, the amount, or the type of purchase or sale. The deadline for filing the quarterly transaction report is 45 days after the end of the quarter, and you should check the correction path in the official system, because the form and the notices change. If that deadline has passed, do not leave the discrepancy unresolved: check the status on the Iranian National Tax Administration portal, or ask the adviser on that file to settle the right path before the matter reaches a tax assessment notice (barg-e tashkhis).

When you file the quarterly transaction report and then find that the buyer's identifier is off by one digit, or that an invoice amount does not agree with the books, it is natural to think of the penalty first. In our advisory work we have often seen that the main trouble does not come from the small mistake itself. It comes from the taxpayer mixing it up with the VAT return, or searching the taxpayer portal for a test invoice and issuing another real invoice instead. If you are looking only for a ready-made sentence that will delete the error, this page does not have one. The error has to be found in that same quarter, the deadline has to be taken from the official calendar, and the correction has to be made through the path the system shows that day, not from what someone remembers of a messaging-group chat.

The quarterly transaction report is not the VAT return

These two obligations look alike because both are tied to the quarter, but they do not cover the same subject. The VAT return concerns the tax and duties for that period, and the filing deadline is 15 days after the end of the quarter. The transaction list under Article 169 has a separate deadline: 45 days after the end of the quarter. If you complete both in one sitting, sales from one may be copied into the other, and you will not later know where the difference came from.

Before any correction, check the tax obligations calendar to see which deadline for that quarter is still open. Read the form and the call-up notice on the Iranian National Tax Administration information portal and the Iranian National Tax Administration. Last year's screenshot and a message in a colleagues' group are not a source for the deadline.

If sales and VAT credit still do not reconcile, close that file separately. We have set out the method in the article Quarterly VAT return: reconcile sales and credit before you file so these two obligations are not mixed in one file.

Do not take coverage from a colleague's line of work

It should not be said that every company or every business files this list. The obligation is phased and depends on the call-up notice and the type of activity. If an obligation has been opened for you in the taxpayer portal, take that as your criterion. You can see the official text of the laws on the National Laws and Regulations System, but coverage of your own file is not settled by one general sentence.

Mistakes to cross off before you file

If you cross off these patterns before filing, a large part of any later correction falls away. We have seen them often in review files, and they usually come from haste or from copying the previous quarter's file.

Counterparty identifier

The most common error is one transposed digit in the counterparty identifier. The system sometimes accepts the record, and the discrepancy surfaces later, when the other party files its own list or when matching begins. Take the identifier from the card, the contract, or an official inquiry, not from a customer message or an old letterhead. For a legal entity, do not substitute the company national ID for the manager's national ID code.

Amount, date, and type of transaction

The VAT-inclusive amount is entered instead of the amount excluding VAT, or the reverse. The invoice date falls in the first days of the next quarter but is recorded in the previous quarter. A purchase appears on a sales row because the software export swapped the column. The total of the rows must agree with the books for that quarter. If it does not, stop the filing until you find the differing line. Whether each field on the form wants the gross or the net amount, read that from the guide to the same form. We do not set a fixed template here, because the form changes.

Omissions and mix-ups with invoices

Some transactions are struck from the list because the buyer's name is not at hand. Others come in from the invoice file and are entered again by hand. A Type 1 electronic invoice carries full seller and buyer details, is used for sales to economic operators, and gives the buyer a tax credit. Type 2 makes buyer details optional, is usually a sale to the final consumer, and carries no tax credit. Type 3 is a card-reader or payment-gateway receipt and carries no tax credit. Do not treat this distinction as the same thing as the transaction-list obligation. The absence of a buyer's name is not, by itself, permission to drop the transaction.

If the scope of invoice issuance is still unclear to you, read it separately in the guide to electronic invoices and who must issue them, and only then decide what goes into this list. The obligation to issue invoices has been rolled out in phases and by call-up notice; do not assume every business is obliged today.

Another costly error is issuing an invoice in the main taxpayer portal just to test. There is no test invoice in the main taxpayer portal, and every invoice issued is real. This list is not corrected by issuing a new invoice.

A worked example: how we reconcile a hypothetical quarter

This scenario is a hypothetical example; the name and figures do not refer to a real taxpayer. Suppose a seller of office supplies notices two errors a few days after filing the spring list. In one sales row, the buyer's national ID is off by one digit. In one purchase row, the amount was taken from the card receipt, while the books show a different figure for that purchase.

Set the three columns side by side

Set three lists side by side: the books for that quarter, invoices issued and received, and the rows that actually went into the report. In this hypothetical example, only those two differences should appear. An extra difference means the statement is still incomplete, and you should not move on to a correction.

Name each error separately

The first error is an identifier error: the counterparty is the same, and only the number was entered wrong. The second is an amount error: the transaction is real, but the figure on the list does not match the figure in the books. Do not lump the two together in a general note such as a spring correction. For each line, write what was filed, what should be filed, and put the invoice number beside it. If you do not know which figure a field on the form wants, read the form guide and do not guess.

Close the correction inside that same period

If you are still within the 45 days after the end of that quarter, make the correction through the path the taxpayer portal shows for that period. After you submit, total the three columns again. Total purchases, total sales, and the number of rows must match what you intended to file. Keep the before and after versions in that quarter's folder so that, if a question comes later, you do not reconstruct the story from memory.

If, in this hypothetical example, the deadline has expired, the seller should not issue a new invoice to make up for it, invent a penalty rate, or mix the row into the next quarter so the totals appear to balance. They must check in the system whether a correction is still possible for that period, and read the latest notice on the Iranian National Tax Administration information portal. This hypothetical example is not a ruling on your file.

If the deadline has passed or the discrepancy is serious

The end of the deadline does not mean the error is wiped out, and it does not mean you may delete the row through an informal channel. First check the status of that period in the taxpayer portal and read the latest notice. If the identifier or amount discrepancy is large, act before the Administration's matching begins. Do not take a penalty figure from this page; rely only on the official source for that year.

If a tax assessment notice (barg-e tashkhis) arrives, do not count the deadline by guesswork. Under Article 238 of the Direct Taxes Act, you have 30 days from the date of service to attend or object, and after that the notice becomes final. Before writing any letter, see the first step in the guide to the first action after a tax assessment notice, so these 30 days are not used up on incomplete work.

An amended return is possible only until the end of the legal deadline for filing that same return. Do not extend this rule to the transaction list. The return deadline for business owners runs to the end of Khordad of the following year, and legal entities have four months after the end of the financial year; for a year ending in Esfand, that is the end of Tir. Do not mix these dates, in one note, with the 45-day quarterly transaction deadline and the 15-day VAT deadline.

Choosing the simplified route for the annual performance return, including where Note 100 applies, is not the answer to whether you must file the transaction list. Check each obligation separately in the taxpayer portal. Do not treat Note 100 as an exemption from electronic invoicing either.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long after the quarter ends must I file the quarterly transaction report?

The deadline for this report is 45 days after the end of the quarter. Do not confuse it with the 15-day VAT return deadline, and also check the exact day for that quarter in the obligations calendar and the Administration's notice.

I entered the buyer's identifier wrong. Should I issue a new invoice?

No. There is no test invoice in the main taxpayer portal, and every invoice issued is real. Follow up the error in that quarter's own list, through the system's correction path.

Must every business file the quarterly transaction report?

No. This obligation is not the same for every business and every company, and it depends on the call-up notice and the taxpayer's status. Check coverage in the taxpayer portal and the Administration's notices.

If the 45-day deadline has passed, can the list still be corrected?

Whether a correction is still possible after the deadline cannot be stated the same way for every file. Check the status of that period in the system and the latest notice, and if the discrepancy is material, consult the adviser on that file before a tax assessment notice (barg-e tashkhis).

Summary

Do not fix the quarterly transaction report in haste, or by issuing a new invoice. Name the error, reconcile the identifier and the amount with the books and the invoices, and keep the 45-day deadline separate from the 15-day VAT deadline. A correction only means something if the system for that period, and the latest notice, allow it.

If you have several discrepancies at once, or the period is closed and you do not know whether a correction is still open, general advice is not enough. Check that quarter's status in the system, and book a time for a file review from the consultation booking page, or email info@foroughshomar.ir.
