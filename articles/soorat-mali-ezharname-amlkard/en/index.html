Financial Statements for the Income Tax ReturnFinancial Statements for the Income Tax Return

Reconcile financial statements for the income tax return with your books before filing. Practical steps, common errors, and legal-entity deadlines.

18 November 2026

By Dr. Hamed Molatefi, tax adviser · Reviewed 19 November 2026

Short answer

Take the income-tax-return figures from that year’s financial statements — statements whose sales, expenses, inventory, and bank balances agree with the supporting documents. Before you close, reconcile sales to invoices, payroll to the social-security list, inventory to a physical count, and the bank balance to the bank statement, and carry only traceable figures into the return. Legal entities have 4 months after the financial year-end; for a year ending in Esfand, the deadline is the end of Tir. Take current audit-inclusion rules and the latest circular from the Iranian National Tax Administration portal.

If you are a company director or accountant and, close to the due date, you still do not know where the return figures should come from, the starting point is the financial statements for the income tax return — not a table built the night you file. In files we have sat through with taxpayers, the main trouble has been that book profit is one figure, recorded sales are another, and the return explains neither. Here we set out a practical year-end close: what to reconcile to what, which document should stay with each expense, and when to send the return through the official portal. We do not guess rates, the audit-inclusion test, or the wording of a new circular; we refer you to the official system or to an adviser.

What do the financial statements for the income tax return cover?

These statements are the picture of the same year whose tax you are computing. At the core are the statement of financial position and the income statement. They are prepared under accounting standards, and the body that issues those standards is the Audit Organization. The return must not be a second story of the company. Every item of revenue, sales return, and expense should reach a general-ledger account and then these same statements. If the software shows one profit and the draft return shows another, identify which document the difference comes from before you file.

An audit of these statements is not an obligation for every company. Some legal entities must have a certified public accountant’s report, under criteria announced by the Iranian National Tax Administration and the relevant regulations. Do not treat last year’s figure from messaging groups as the test; check current inclusion with the Iranian Association of Certified Public Accountants and the Administration’s portal. If you are in scope and the auditor’s report does not accompany the return, an examination often starts from that gap. If you are not in scope, the internal statements must still hold together. An auditor’s report does not replace invoices, the social-security list, or the bank statement.

Run these three reconciliations before you close the year

Closing the year is not a software command. First see whether the book entries agree with actual sales, actual payments, and the bank and warehouse balances. Complete the three reconciliations below before the closing entry. If one does not agree, do not file the return hoping an oral explanation will do; an explanation without paper usually ends in that same meeting.

Reconcile sales to invoices and the quarterly report

Total book revenue must agree with invoices issued and the quarterly transaction report. The deadline for Article 169 quarterly transactions is 45 days after the end of the quarter, and the VAT return is due within 15 days after the end of the quarter. Differences between these reports and the books land on sales at year-end. If a rollout notice has brought you into electronic invoicing, a sale to an economic operator is Type 1 and gives the buyer input credit; a sale to a consumer is usually Type 2 and carries no credit; and a card-reader or payment-gateway receipt is Type 3, which also carries no credit. Inclusion has been phased and does not cover every company from a single date. See your own rollout notice on the Iranian National Tax Administration information portal.

Do not take an undocumented expense into profit

An expense reduces profit only if it relates to the company’s activity, is ordinary, and is backed by a document you can produce. A software entry alone is not evidence. If you take a purchase into cost of sales and also expect VAT input credit, a paper invoice is not a shortcut; read why a paper purchase invoice does not create a tax credit. Payroll is separate. The social-security list, payroll tax paid, and book wages must agree. Before the closing entry, draw out discrepancies between the social-security list and the books; either correct them or leave the reason in writing with the file.

Bank, inventory, and receivables

The book bank balance must agree with the bank statement. Record uncollected checks, unrecorded charges, and deposits from the last days of the year on the reconciliation, and post any missing entry in the same year, not the next. If part of sales went into the director’s personal account, that amount is not removed; it only becomes harder to trace. That situation is covered in Commercial accounts and bank transactions. Count the inventory and document any shortage or surplus in minutes. Do not reduce receivables with a round provision and no customer name.

A worked example: closing the year for a hypothetical trading company

This example is hypothetical, and the company name is not real. The trading company White Palm has a financial year ending in Esfand, sells goods, and has about 10 people on the payroll. From mid-Esfand, instead of constructing a preferred profit, the accountant worked through four steps in order.

Step one: sales and invoices

The software sales report was set beside the total of electronic invoices issued. The difference was a few credit invoices: the return had been booked, but it had not yet been voided or amended in the Taxpayers’ System (Samaneh-ye Moadian). Before closing, those invoices were resolved so that book sales and recorded sales told one story. No new invoice was issued as a test. The main workspace is no place for a trial, and every invoice issued is real.

The later steps: payroll, warehouse, and bank

In step two, the year’s social-security list was compared with book payroll expense. One person was missing from the list in the early months and was added later. Instead of manipulating profit, the reason was written in a file note. In step three, inventory was counted. The shortage against the stock cards was documented in minutes and shown in cost of sales, not in miscellaneous expenses. In step four, the bank balance was reconciled. Several deposits at the end of Esfand appeared only at the bank, and their entries were posted in the same year. The director’s personal withdrawal was taken to the partners’ current account, not to expense. The trial balance was then agreed to the income statement, and those same figures were carried into the return. In this hypothetical, ignoring the inventory shortage might have produced another number, but every line has a document. An unsupported lower figure is usually more dangerous in an examination than a transparent one.

Errors that weaken the financial statements in an examination

Cutting profit with an adjusting entry on the night of filing, with no underlying event and no paper, is spotted quickly and also undermines confidence in the other figures. Do not take the director’s personal expenses into the income statement either. Cash withdrawals and installments on personal facilities belong in the director’s account or the partners’ current account. The opening balance must also agree with the prior year’s closing balance. If share capital or retained earnings have moved without reason, the assessing officer starts from the balance sheet.

Do not mix liabilities with expenses. Withholding tax, contract social-security contributions, and the performance retention each have their own account and should not be labelled as a single expense. Do not miss the correction window either. An amended return is possible only until the end of the statutory filing deadline for that same return. If the matter reaches a tax assessment notice (barg-e tashkhis), under Article 238 of the Direct Taxes Act you have 30 days from the date of service to attend or object, after which it becomes a final assessment notice (barg-e qati). Those 30 days do not replace the return deadline.

The deadline for legal entities, and how to file

Legal entities file the income tax return within 4 months after the end of the financial year. For a year ending in Esfand, that deadline is the end of Tir of the following year. If the deadline falls on a holiday, take the practical filing day from the tax obligations calendar and from the Administration’s announcement, not from a forwarded message.

Filing is done through the National Tax e-Services Portal, not the Government Single Window. Sign-in uses the national ID code, a password, and an SMS code. Attach the financial statements on the path the portal shows for legal entities. The Taxpayers’ System (Samaneh-ye Moadian) workspace is for invoices, and the main workspace has no test invoice; do not issue a test invoice to make profit agree. Late filing creates a penalty. Read the penalty rate and the conditions for remission in the Administration’s current circular, not in an old figure. An installment plan is a separate path and does not replace a proper year-end close.

Frequently Asked Questions

Must every company file audited financial statements with the return?

No. Audit inclusion depends on criteria announced by the Iranian National Tax Administration and the relevant regulations. Take the current criteria from the Administration’s portal or the Iranian Association of Certified Public Accountants, not from a figure circulating in groups.

What if profit in the financial statements does not match the return?

The assessing officer will ask for an explanation and documents, and an unexplained difference usually leads to expenses being disallowed or income being added. Complete the return from the financial statements. A correction through an amended return is possible only until the end of the statutory deadline for that same return.

When is the income tax return due for a company with an Esfand year-end?

Legal entities have 4 months after the end of the financial year. For a year ending in Esfand, the deadline is the end of Tir of the following year. Check the exact day for that year on the portal and the obligations calendar.

Should we issue a test invoice to check that sales agree with profit?

No. The main workspace of the Taxpayers’ System (Samaneh-ye Moadian) has no test invoice, and every invoice issued is real. Reconcile using the sales report and the invoices already issued.

Summary

Financial statements for the income tax return are useful when sales, expenses, inventory, and the bank all come from one set of books, and the return is a defensible copy of those books. You have 4 months after the financial year-end; spend that time closing discrepancies, not manufacturing a figure.
