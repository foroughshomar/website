Simple Guide to Registering in the Taxpayers' System for Small BusinessesSimple Guide to Registering in the Taxpayers' System for Small Businesses

A practical, step-by-step guide to registering in the Taxpayers' System designed specifically for small and medium-sized businesses, including useful tips and solutions to common problems.

23 October 2026

By Dr. Hamed Molatefi, tax adviser · Reviewed 26 October 2026

The Taxpayers' System (Samaneh-ye Moadian) is one of the most significant transformations in Iran's tax system in recent years. This system was designed to reduce paperwork, increase transparency, and streamline tax processes, and now almost all businesses are required to register in it. For owners of small businesses, who typically have limited time and resources, registering in this system can sometimes seem daunting. However, the reality is that with a little proper guidance, the process is entirely manageable.

In this article, we explain the registration steps for small businesses in a practical way without unnecessary complications. Our goal is that after reading this text, you can enter the system with complete confidence and register your information.

What is the Taxpayers' System and why is it important for your small business?

The Taxpayers' System is a platform through which the Iranian National Tax Administration communicates with taxpayers. In this system, electronic invoices are issued, purchases and sales are recorded, and tax returns are processed in a smarter and faster manner.

For small businesses, being present in this system means greater transparency in compliance with the law and, consequently, a reduced likelihood of fines and future disputes. In addition, many new tax facilities and exemptions can only be obtained through this system. Therefore, timely registration is not only a legal requirement but also an opportunity to better manage your business's taxes.

Preparing documents before starting registration

Before entering the system, it is better to prepare a few documents and pieces of information so that the process proceeds without interruption. You will typically need the business owner's identity information, a valid phone number and email, the exact postal code of the business location, details of the bank account related to economic activities, and, if applicable, business licenses or establishment permits.

If your business is a legal entity, company documents including the articles of association, latest registration changes, and introduction of a representative will also be required. We recommend saving all this information in an organized file so it is easily accessible when needed.

Registering on the Tax Administration's e-services portal (my.tax.gov.ir)

Registration starts on the Iranian National Tax Administration's own e-services portal, my.tax.gov.ir, not on a general government portal. If you have already registered there (for example for a tax return or an economic code), log in with that account.

If you do not have an account, register with your national ID number, date of birth and mobile number. The mobile number must be registered in your own name (or the company's legal representative), because the one-time password is sent to it. Later logins use your national ID, a password you set yourself, and an SMS code.

Steps to register in the Taxpayers' System

After logging in to my.tax.gov.ir, first complete your tax file registration if you do not yet have a tax file and economic code. Then use "Enter dashboard" to open your Taxpayers' System dashboard (tp.tax.gov.ir) and select the tax file you want to activate.

At this stage, carefully enter the identity information, address, type of economic activity, start date of operations, and other requested details. For small businesses, simpler options are usually displayed based on your scale of operations.

After completing the information, the system will review your request. This process may take a few minutes to a few hours. During this time, it is best to keep the page open or check your email.

Common problems and practical solutions

Many small businesses encounter issues during identity verification or document upload. If the system displays an "incomplete information" error, double-check all fields. Sometimes an extra space or typo can cause this error.

If the verification code is not sent, recheck the mobile number or use the resend option. In special cases where the system does not allow registration, the best solution is to contact the Taxpayers' System support or visit the nearest tax office.

Businesses that were previously registered in older systems may have their previous information transferred automatically. In this case, you only need to verify and update the information.

What to do after registration?

To issue invoices, obtain a unique tax memory ID (shenaseh-ye yekta-ye hafezeh-ye maliati) under "Tax file and membership" in your dashboard. While doing so you must choose how invoices will be sent: directly (you or your software sign and send them with your own private key), through a trusted tax service provider (with the provider's key or your own), or through government systems. Only one sending method can be active per unique ID at a time. Keep your password and private key safe.

Warning: there are no "test invoices" in the live dashboard. Every invoice you issue and register is real and affects your tax position and the buyer's. To practise, use your software's or trusted provider's sandbox, or issue your first real invoices carefully with an adviser. Many accounting programs are connected to the Taxpayers' System and can make the work easier.

Conclusion

Registration in the Taxpayers' System is no longer an option for small businesses but a necessity. With a little initial preparation and following the steps in order, this task can be completed easily and will help improve transparency and better tax management for your business in the long term.

If you encounter any doubts at any stage or the system behaves unexpectedly, seek guidance from reputable tax consultants or refer directly to the official website of the Iranian National Tax Administration. Starting on time and correctly will protect you from many future problems and penalties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the steps to register in the Muodian system for small businesses?

Registration in the Muodian system is done through the national portal of electronic government services (single window). First enter the single window system, then select the option related to the tax organization and the Muodian system. Enter identity information, address, type of economic activity, start date of activity and other details so that the system reviews your request.

What documents are needed to register in the Muodian system?

Before entering the system, prepare the business owner's identity information, valid phone number and email, exact postal code of the business location, bank account information related to economic activity, and if available, business licenses or establishment permits. If the business is legal, company documents including articles of association, latest registration changes and representative introduction will also be required.

What is the Muodian system and why is it important for small businesses?

The Muodian system is a platform through which the tax organization communicates with taxpayers, where electronic invoices are issued, purchases and sales are recorded, and tax returns are processed in a smarter and faster way. For small businesses, being in this system means more transparency towards the law and reduces the possibility of fines. Many new tax facilities and exemptions can only be received through this system.
